usage: swarmq schedule prune [-h] --orphans [--format {table,json}]

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --orphans             Remove static crons without any report from a worker
                        (legacy entries, apps before 0.10.0). The leader never
                        removes them on its own. A disabled cron comes back
                        disabled when a worker reports it again.
  --format {table,json}
                        Output format. Default: 'table' on a TTY, 'json' on a
                        pipe. Override the default with
                        SWARMQ_DEFAULT_FORMAT=table|json.
